Changelog (Last Update: 09.05.2026 - Version 3.2.3)

Version 3.2.3 - Evil Petals from Reisen's petals recipe cost one more Nightmare Fuel. Dried Evil Petals crafting now requires Shadow Manipulator–tier magic prototyping rather than improvised no-station work.
  [中文] 铃仙制作的邪恶花瓣多消耗 1 份噩梦燃料；风干邪恶花瓣改为需暗影操纵者档次的暗影魔法原型台解锁，不能再仅靠空手配方。

Version 3.2.2 - Lunatic Vision Ribbon: with a Shadow or Possessed Shadow Atrium socketed, the soft glow is a gentler lilac tint, scales with effective low Sanity as a share of your maximum pool (not a single fixed raw-Sanity band), and only appears at surface night or while underground; at full drain it still reaches about a 2.5-radius pool of light. Possessed sockets still mint a Dark Petal every two auto-consumed fuel ticks, and scrapbook lines now spell out embedded night sight. Dried Evil Petals crafting gets matching recipe flavour text (4 Nightmare Fuel, 1 Cut Grass). Dodging no longer flashes a brief hit-stun state on distant clients; AoE lookups for Mind Blowing and boosted PICK/HARVEST/PICKUP—and the boosted aura effect—stay on lighter server paths. Nightmare Amulet, Alter Guardian Hat, and similar overrides funnel through one effective-Sanity helper so Mind Blowing no longer trims Sanity beneath a zero HUD reading. Creatures shaken by Mind Blowing fear it for a shorter window. Moon Port asks for a fraction longer pause between hops. Under Enlightenment, Mind Blowing and Moon Port use the same effective Enlightenment Sanity you see for “full sanity” spillover checks, including induced-insanity quirks, before extra cost rolls into Hunger.
  [中文] 幻视丝带镶嵌暗影心房或附身心房：柔和光晕改为偏淡丁香色，按相对理智上限的“有效理智”缩放，地表白昼不亮、洞穴与地表夜间随档位亮起，理智归零时半径约可达 2.5；附身心房仍可每两份自动消耗的燃料掉落一枚邪恶花瓣；图鉴说明补充镶嵌夜视等描述。风干邪恶花瓣（4 噩梦燃料 + 1 茎草）补了配方品鉴句。远程端闪避不再闪受击；心灵风暴及强化采集等的范围检索与强化光环服务端更轻；噩梦护符、异化守护者帽子等理智覆盖统一走有效理智函数，HUD 归零时心灵风暴不会再暗扣理智。受心灵风暴恐惧的单位持续时间略缩短。月之相位连续施放最短间隔略加长。启蒙状态下，心灵风暴与月之相位在判定“理智已满、溢出转自饥饿”时改为按你画面上看到的启蒙理智（含诱因型疯狂）来算，避免因隐藏数值差一口气。

Version 3.2.1 - Release Mind Blowing and Moon Port now use dedicated cast animations so online stays in sync and you recover from the cast lock sooner; Moon Port hides briefly before the blink, refuses bad landings, uses a short cooldown, clamps ground targeting range unless you are boosted, and usually needs Lunatic combo layers to cast. Enlightenment-affecting gear now lines up with Sanity tiers, Lunar Battle Uniform move and knockback, molting checks, and low-Sanity work hints; near full Enlightenment, overflow ability cost pays in Hunger instead of feeling free. Boosted monster-meat fallout waits a beat so finishing a bite stays smooth.
  [中文] 释放心灵风暴与月之相位使用独立施法，联机更同步、更快恢复可操作；月之相位短暂隐匿后传送，不可走点无效，带短冷却，未强化时限距、强化后更远，通常需先有连击层数才可用。启蒙类道具与理智档位、月夜制服移速及抗击退、换毛判定、低理智劳作提示一致；接近满启蒙时溢出消耗转自饥饿。强化怪物肉触发略延后，避免与进食动作冲突。

Version 3.2.0 - Release Mind Blowing and Moon Port heal nearby allies; on PvP, hostile players take Mind Blowing damage at the vanilla rate and can be slowed by Mind Stopper. Players are never feared. Release can target survivors; PvP no longer stacks or charges off player fights (epic kills pay more); boosted crit visuals and re-trigger refreshed; walls, structures, and veggie plants do not add release charge.
  [中文] 释放心灵风暴与月之相位治疗友军；PvP 下敌对玩家受心灵风暴伤害（原版比例）并可被心灵制止减速；玩家不会被恐惧。可对幸存者施放；PvP 中对阵玩家不再叠层或蓄能（史诗击杀加成更高）；强化暴击与再触发表现更新；墙体、建筑与蔬菜类击杀不计蓄能。
